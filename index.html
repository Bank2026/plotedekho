REASONING ABILITY & ANALYTICAL APTITUDE

MODULE 1: SYLLOGISMS ("ONLY A FEW" & POSSIBILITY)

  • "Only a few A are B": Dual simultaneous condition: "Some A are B" AND "Some A are NOT B". "All A being B is a possibility" is ALWAYS FALSE.
  • "Only A are B": Equivalent to "All B are A", where B cannot overlap with any other element.

ENGLISH LANGUAGE & VERBAL ABILITY

MODULE 1: SUBJECT-VERB AGREEMENT & GOLDEN RULES

  • When subjects are joined by along with, as well as, together with, in addition to, the verb agrees with the FIRST SUBJECT.
  • When subjects are joined by Either...or, Neither...nor, the verb agrees with the NEAREST SUBJECT.
